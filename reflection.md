The GUI collects purchase data, validates the email and price (at most $1,000), converts it to JSON, and sends it to the API with the POST request. The API checks the data again and replies, and the GUI shows the response. The form also has a checkbox to mark whether the purchase was online or in store. I was surprised at how much of the work was validation. My code evolved from the example's name, email, and age form into a purchase form, and I added the checks to the API. I understand JSON as a shared contract between programs better now. I would like to learn more about databases and authentication.
